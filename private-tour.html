The experience

More intimate, more flexible, better adapted

A private tour is not just the normal route with fewer people. It is a different product: curated pace, more dialogue, route decisions made around your group and space to go deeper where it matters.

01

Tailored route

Historic center, Jewish Quarter, quieter canals, family-friendly pace or a more cinematic approach. The route starts from your priorities.

02

Flexible timing

Morning, afternoon or sunset depending on availability, with space to adapt to your travel plan and energy.

03

Closer conversation

More room for questions, recommendations, local context and practical advice without the constraints of a large group.
