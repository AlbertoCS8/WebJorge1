About the company

Nuevo Mundo was created to tell Amsterdam with more care and more intention

Behind the tours there is a broader idea: building experiences that feel elegant, human and well crafted, whether they are public walks, private routes or collaborations with creators and brands.

Read our story
What defines us
  • Carefully designed experiences instead of generic routes
  • A strong narrative voice rooted in local history and style
  • Direct relationship with each traveler, team or collaborator

Our story

A personal project that became a brand with its own point of view

Nuevo Mundo grows from Jorge's way of guiding: close but refined, entertaining but rigorous, and always with the intention of making people feel the city rather than just consume it.

01

Craft over volume

The company is not built around doing as many tours as possible. It is built around making each route, page and collaboration feel intentional.

02

A recognizable style

History, local culture, aesthetics and conversation come together in a tone that is more editorial than touristy.

03

Flexible growth

The project can host travelers, private groups and media collaborations without losing its voice, because the core idea stays the same.

What we do

Three lines of work, one way of understanding the city

Public tours

Accessible routes with personality

Shared tours designed to introduce Amsterdam properly, without flattening its contrasts or history.

Private tours

Tailored experiences

Routes adapted to pace, interests and context, with a more intimate relationship and direct booking flow.

Media

Support for creators and brands

Local production support, storytelling and routes with visual and narrative weight for content and campaigns.

Philosophy

We do not sell Amsterdam as a postcard. We frame it as a living city.

That means there is room for beauty, but also context. Room for memorable corners, but also for stories, tensions and everyday details that make the experience feel real.

Nuevo Mundo is the structure that allows that approach to grow with coherence.
