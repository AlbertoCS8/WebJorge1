About me

My name is Jorge and we are going to enjoy together a dynamic, fun, and above all interesting tour! I am originally from Madrid, Spain. Passionate about history and the world of travel. I stand out for making you see the city in a different way. If I live in Amsterdam, it is because of a love story. In this case, I did not fall in love with a woman... I fell in love with the city. It was the year 2008. As a child, I was taking a trip in Belgium with my parents visiting the main cities. One day we decided to escape to Amsterdam. On this trip, I was able to see the city for a day and I certainly fell in love. Sixteen years have passed since that moment and today I live in this city which I am completely in love with, as well as its history, its streets, and its people. I hope to make you vibrate on this tour. We will discover together the past and present of Amsterdam. Join me on this adventure. We will have a great time!

My tours are free (tips appreciated!) and designed to give you an authentic Amsterdam experience. Whether you're interested in history, culture, or just finding the best local spots, I've got you covered.

Join me for a walk you won't forget!
